How to play
  • Drag to move. Your paddle swings by itself when the ball is in reach.
  • At the kitchen line you dink. From the back, stand still for a soft drop or move into the ball to drive.
  • Two-bounce rule: the serve and the return must bounce before anyone volleys.
  • No volleys from the kitchen. Step away from balls that are flying out.
  • Doubles: both partners serve before a side out. The score reads serving team, other team, then server 1 or 2.
  • Online: one phone hosts and shares the code. Friends open the same link and join. Empty spots are CPU players.
